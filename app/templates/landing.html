How it works

One pipeline, every request — the same five stages you'll see traced live inside the app.

1

Observe

Gathers context: your documents, chat history, and anything on screen you've shared.

2

Understand

Resolves follow-ups and classifies what you actually want — a question, a summary, an email, an export.

3

Analyze

Routes to the right document and retrieves the exact passages that matter.

4

Guide

Generates a grounded answer, then a second agent independently validates every claim in it.

5

Automate

For action requests, executes it directly — a real draft, a real file, ready to download.
